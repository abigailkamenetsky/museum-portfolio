A vast, wide, soaring dark academia baroque picture gallery inside a European
palace. Everything in this room is DARK, moody and dimly lit. This is a
shadowed, candlelit hall at dusk, not a bright daylit museum. Deep shadows fill
the corners, the vault and the far end of the room, and only pools of warm
candlelight and shafts of cool window light break the darkness.

The walls are DEEP DARK FOREST GREEN, hex #0F1B14 and #1B2A1F, a near-black
green like dark bottle glass or deep pine needles in shadow. The walls must NOT
be sage, NOT mint, NOT olive, NOT grey-green, NOT pale green, NOT bright green
and NOT light green. They are almost black in the corners. A subtle tonal
green-on-green damask pattern is barely visible in the wall silk. Below the
green runs a low dark wood and cream marble wainscot with restrained gilding.

The floor is VERY DARK WALNUT herringbone parquet, hex #2A1E17 and #3A2F24,
a deep near-black brown wood, polished so candlelight reflects along it. The
floor must NOT be light wood, NOT honey oak, NOT golden brown, NOT pale. Down
the centre lies a long runner carpet in DARK FOREST GREEN, hex #1B2A1F, with
deep oxblood red #A63D2D borders and an intricate woven medallion pattern. The
carpet green must be dark, not pale or mint.

The walls carry MANY separate gold picture frames, arranged in a dense salon
hang: several different frames on each wall section between the windows, in
mixed sizes, some large and some small, hung in rows two or three high. They
are modest carved gilt frames, NOT enormous, NOT one single giant frame per
wall. Every frame is completely EMPTY, containing only flat plain dark neutral
canvas with no image, no painting, no portrait, no figure and no pattern.
Gilded candle sconces are mounted on the green wall BETWEEN and BESIDE the
frames, never inside a frame and never overlapping a frame.

There is EXACTLY ONE stained glass window in the entire room, centred on the far
end wall. Every window along the two side walls is ORNATE BUT CLEAR GLASS with
leaded clear panes and carved stone surrounds, showing green trees and daylight
outside. The side windows must NOT be stained glass and must NOT be coloured.

The single stained glass window on the far end wall is a tall arched leaded
window in the style of Hieronymus Bosch and the Garden of Earthly Delights: a dense medieval
fantasy scene of many small pale nude figures, fantastical creatures, birds,
strange fruit, blue and green glass orbs and spheres, and a large central
circular globe medallion, all rendered in small leaded panes of deep jewel
colours, dark blues, forest greens, oxblood reds and amber. It is antique,
intricate, medieval and painterly. It must NOT be modern, NOT abstract, NOT
simple, NOT geometric and must not contain large flat blocks of colour.

The ceiling is a vast barrel vault, high overhead, in PALE AGED CREAM AND
OFF-WHITE PLASTER, hex #C8BDA4 and #7D6A52. The vault is mostly pale plaster,
roughly three quarters cream and one quarter gilding. Antique gold, hex
#6B5E3E, appears only as restrained ACCENT on the carved ribs, the rosettes,
the bead moulding and the panel frame edges. The vault must NOT be
predominantly gold, NOT brassy, NOT bronze, NOT a gold ceiling. It reads as
carved white and cream plasterwork with gilded highlights, like aged stucco.
It is densely covered in deeply carved rinceau scrollwork, acanthus leaves,
rosettes and raised plaster ornament, divided into panels by carved transverse
ribs.

Set into the vault between the ribs is a row of large rectangular recessed
panels, each surrounded by carved gilt frame moulding. Every one of these
ceiling panels is EMPTY: the interior is flat plain dark neutral canvas with no
painting, no fresco, no figures, no angels, no clouds and no scene of any kind.
Leave the ceiling panels blank.

There is EXACTLY ONE door in the entire room: a single tall wooden double door
with a carved pediment above it, centred on the end wall OPPOSITE the stained
glass window. There are no other doors anywhere. The side walls have no doors,
no doorways and no openings other than the clear windows.

Tall arched clear windows line both side walls with cool daylight and green
trees outside.

The long patterned carpet runs down the centre of the floor for the length of
the hall, and low dark wood benches upholstered in deep oxblood red velvet sit
along the side walls. Gilded bronze candelabra and ornate wall sconces holding
lit candles are mounted on the walls, and a gilded console table with a marble
top stands against one wall. These furnishings are crisply modelled with clean
edges and realistic brass, gilt, velvet and wood.

Apart from the carpet and those benches, the room contains NO SCULPTURE OF ANY
KIND: no statues, no marble figures, no busts, no carved heads, no pedestals
and no plinths, anywhere in the room. Nobody and nothing stands upright in the
middle of the hall. The walkable centre of the room is clear from one end to
the other, with only the carpet on the floor.
